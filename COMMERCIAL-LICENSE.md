## Why this arrangement

AutoAB is released openly so it can be inspected, cited and built on in
research, which is where it came from. The AGPL keeps that openness intact: it
prevents the work being absorbed into a closed product without anything flowing
back. Anyone who prefers not to accept those terms is welcome to license it
commercially instead.
